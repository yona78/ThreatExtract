Threat Intelligence Report — 2024-03-15

On 2024-03-15, the APT29 group (also known as Cozy Bear) launched a spear-phishing
campaign against several government agencies. The threat actors deployed the
WellMess malware and the SUNBURST backdoor to establish persistence.

Command-and-control traffic was observed to the domain evil-c2.example.com and to
the IP address 203.0.113.42. The attackers exploited CVE-2021-44228 (Log4Shell) in
vulnerable Apache Log4j servers to gain initial access.

Indicators of compromise include the file hash 5d41402abc4b2a76b9719d911017c592
and the malicious URL http://malicious.example.net/payload.exe. The SecTeam at
CERT-EU attributed the activity to the SVR and recommended verification with the
Nmap and Wireshark tools.
